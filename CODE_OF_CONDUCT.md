# Code of Conduct

Be respectful and professional. Harassment, personal attacks, and
discriminatory conduct are not tolerated in issues, PRs, reviews, or chat.

Reports: contact the maintainers privately (see `SECURITY.md` for the
reporting channel until the first public release). Enforcement: maintainers
may remove comments, block contributions, or ban accounts for violations.

The full Contributor Covenant text may be adopted in a later wave; the
standard above is binding from M0.
